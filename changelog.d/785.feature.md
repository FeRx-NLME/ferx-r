- **`ferx_simulate_adaptive(reference = "fixed")` reports the controller's
  effect as a contrast** (#785, ferx-core #946): the same patients are run
  under the `[adaptive_dosing]` rules and under the block with its rules
  cleared (the start dose at every due point), on one seed, so both arms share
  every random number. Every table gains a leading `ARM` column
  (`"adaptive"`, `"reference"`, and on the summary and decomposition tables
  `"difference"`, the paired adaptive - reference effect); `arm_labels =` names
  the arms, and an empty label is refused, coded `E_ADAPTIVE_OPTIONS`.
