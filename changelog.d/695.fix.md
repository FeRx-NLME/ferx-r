- **`ferx_xpose()` carries time-varying covariates to the right subject when
  `fit$sdtab`'s IDs do not line up with `fit$covtab`'s.** (#695) The covariate
  join now places each observation by its `fit$subject_summary` position.
  Two adjacent subjects whose IDs parse to one number (`"1"`, then `"01"`) lost
  the second subject's covariates to `NA`, and a subject with no Gaussian
  observations sent the whole join back to the raw ID, which merged the
  records of a reused ID and gave `NA` for every non-numeric ID.
