- **ferx now builds against ferx-core `d66c0c51`**, up from `5bd862b8`
  (#655). These engine changes reach R:

  - **A `[mixture]` fit records its fitted per-class overrides**
    ([ferx-core #1765](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/1765),
    [#2361](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2361)). The new
    `fit$mixture_overrides` holds one class variance per `omega(k)` override and
    one class sigma per `sigma(k)` override, for every estimator, SAEM
    included. `ferx_save_fit()` / `ferx_load_fit()` keep it bit for bit, and
    `ferx_sir()` and `ferx_covariance()` read it ahead of
    `fit$packed_estimate`, so they now work on an SAEM mixture fit (which has
    no packed estimate), where they refused before. A fit that carries
    neither is refused with `E_FIT_INPUT_MISSING`.
    `ferx_simulate_with_uncertainty()` simulates such a fit as well (#671).
    An SAEM fit with an `omega(k)` override also now
    uses that override as the class's prior during the iterations and in the
    final OFV, EBEs and covariance step; it used the base Omega. Estimates of
    such fits change.
  - **IOV fits warm-start every inner solve at the previous mode, kappa included,
    and the inner BFGS converges from a start next to the mode**
    ([ferx-core #2261](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2261),
    [#2271](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2271),
    [#2374](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2374);
    [#2270](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2270),
    [#2280](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2280)). FOCE/FOCEI,
    SAEM and VI final EBEs, SIR draws, the `agq_eval_only` stage, profiled fits
    and the covariance step reach the same optimum with far fewer inner
    evaluations (warfarin: 143,927 to 18,424). Reconverged finite-difference
    gradients re-solve the EBEs at an inner tolerance of at most `1e-7`, so FD
    gradients, and IOV results generally, can move in their trailing digits.
  - **A profiled fit is reported converged only once its stop is verified**
    ([ferx-core #2285](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2285),
    [#2338](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2338)). For a model
    with a `profile`d level block under L-BFGS or SLSQP, a fresh leg from the
    best point must lower the OFV by no more than `1e-6`, at about 5-20 more
    objective evaluations per fit. `mbma_placebo` with its `PLACEBO` block
    profiled now ends 7.8e-9 below the unprofiled OFV (was 6.6e-7 above).
  - **A profiled fit's not-converged warnings are convergence warnings**
    ([ferx-core #2341](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2341),
    [#2364](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2364)). The profiled
    optimizer's "stopped without converging", leg-cap and spent-budget
    warnings, and BOBYQA's polish-leg-cap warning, are `critical` /
    `convergence` in `ferx_get_warnings()` (were `warning` / `general`), with
    the convergence guidance.
  - **A profiled fit does the housekeeping an unprofiled one does**
    ([ferx-core #2136](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2136),
    [#2353](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2353)):
    `optimizer_trace = TRUE` now writes a trace that `ferx_trace()` and
    `plot(fit)` read (it was empty), checkpoints are written, a structural
    theta the objective never reads is frozen with the usual warning, and
    `covariance_fallback = "sir"` gets a proposal when the Hessian is not
    positive definite.
  - **A profiled block under a global `sum_to_zero` is solved as a bordered
    constraint**
    ([ferx-core #2135](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2135),
    [#2355](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2355)): one profile
    evaluation on a 100-study placebo model drops from 85 s to 1.5 s.
  - **`ferx_model_validate(model, data)` reports each nested level**
    ([ferx-core #2035](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2035),
    [#2349](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2349)): one
    `W_LEVEL_SUMMARY` row per level gives its group count, groups per parent
    (min / median / max), how many parents hold a single group, and the kappas
    at that level. The row is informational, but the engine has no
    informational severity, so it is listed as a warning and the result reads
    "VALID (with warnings)". `ferx_fit()` does not record it.
  - **A blank level cell is refused with `E_LEVEL_MISSING_LABEL`**
    ([ferx-core #2036](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2036),
    [#2351](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2351)): a record with
    an empty level column used to silently join the previous record's group.
    `ferx_fit()`, `ferx_model_validate()` and the simulate functions refuse it.
  - **A covariate change on a TTE or CTMM row counts as time-varying**
    ([ferx-core #1854](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/1854),
    [#2314](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2314)), and a
    drug-driven `[markov_model]` whose PK disposition reads a time-varying
    covariate is refused with `E_ENDPOINT_TV_COVARIATE` instead of being read
    at the baseline value. Some models accepted before are now refused.
  - **Saved fits from earlier versions keep loading, but their population
    fingerprint is no longer compared** (ferx-core #1854, #2036: fingerprint
    scheme 6). `ferx_sir()` / `ferx_covariance()` on such a fit run as before
    and add a note to the result's `warnings` that the population was not
    verified.
  - **Level-block binding fixes**: a block with some levels `FIX` is no longer
    falsely refused under `sum_to_zero`
    ([ferx-core #1992](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/1992),
    [#2363](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2363)), and on an
    ODE model the absorption check counts a kappa's carry-over into later
    occasions, so some identified models it refused now bind
    ([ferx-core #1877](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/1877),
    [#2360](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2360)).
  - **SIR on a `[mixture]` model is about ten times faster**
    ([ferx-core #1769](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/1769),
    [#2359](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2359)): each draw
    warm-starts every class at its EBEs; the draw at the estimates still
    equals the fit's OFV exactly.
  - **Warning fixes**: `W_STEADY_STATE_ABSOLUTE_TIME` no longer fires on a
    joint PK-TTE model whose hazard reads time through an `[odes]`
    intermediate
    ([ferx-core #1279](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/1279),
    [#2345](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2345)), and the
    FOCE-M3 non-interaction warning now fires when the method chain ends in an
    evaluation-only stage such as `imp_eval_only`
    ([ferx-core #1726](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/1726),
    [#2352](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2352)).
