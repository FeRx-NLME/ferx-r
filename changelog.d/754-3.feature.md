- **`ferx_calc_npde()` scores single-event time-to-event records** (#754,
  #210, [ferx-core #1889](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/1889)).
  On a TTE row `NPDE` and `NPD` now hold the record's simulation-based npde
  (the same number in both): an observed event is scored by the fraction of
  replicates with an earlier event, a right-censored time is imputed between
  that fraction and 1. They equal the scores a fit with
  `settings = list(npde_nsim = , npde_seed = )` writes, and they survive
  `ferx_save_fit()`. Repeated events, competing risks, left truncation and
  interval-censored records are not scored: their rows stay `NA` and a
  `W_NPDE_TTE_SKIPPED` warning says why. `EPRED` / `PWRES` stay `NA` on TTE
  rows.
