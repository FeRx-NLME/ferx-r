- **Results and output that change with ferx-core `1caa2923..a959f4b6`** (#741):

  - **Durbin-Watson values move on models with occasions that run in time.**
    `fit$dw_statistic` and `fit$iwres_lag1_r` split a subject's residual
    series at its occasions only when they are parallel (study arms, level
    cells, which restart the clock). Occasions that follow one another in time
    (dosing intervals, study periods) are one series again, whether an IOV
    column or an `iov_occasion` rule declares them. Since ferx-core #2798
    every occasion ended a series, so a design with one observation per
    occasion, such as sparse TDM, had no statistic (`NA`) and no warning; it
    has both again (the bundled `warfarin_iov` at its initial estimates: 0.13
    to 0.42). On a compartment-free model the warning names the IOV column
    only when the statistic was split there
    ([ferx-core #2829](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2829)).
  - **A variance the optimizer stopped just above its internal lower guard is
    resumed from the guard** when the objective is lower there, instead of
    being reported `converged = TRUE` at a point that is not a minimum. It
    applies after every outer optimizer; derivative-free BOBYQA, which every
    `[mixture]` fit runs, is where it was seen (a class-2 override stopping
    0.159 OFV above the optimum). The resumed fit carries an
    `optimizer_health` warning naming the variance; one that ends no lower,
    or stops short of a guard again, is `converged = FALSE`
    ([ferx-core #2832](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2832)).
  - **`fit$n_iterations` counts the probe evaluation** a fit spends when it
    stops with a theta on the internal lower floor, also when the probe finds
    no lower objective and no restart follows. No estimate changes
    ([ferx-core #2850](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2850)).
