- **Engine fixes from ferx-core `32dbb309..b259dd10`** (#669). Estimates,
  OFVs or standard errors change for the models each item names:

  - **The objective no longer depends on the units of `DV`**
    ([ferx-core #2441](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2441),
    [#2443](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2443)): residual
    variances were floored at an absolute `1e-12`, so under proportional error
    a prediction near zero, or an additive `sigma <= 1e-6`, was scored at the
    floor. Only a variance that cannot be scored (`NaN`, `<= 0`, subnormal)
    is replaced now. Such fits change their OFV, toward NONMEM's.
  - **SAEM, IMP, IMPMAP and VI score a negative prediction as it is**
    ([ferx-core #2440](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2440),
    [#2444](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2444)), instead of
    clamping it to `1e-12`, so a baseline-minus-effect or change-from-baseline
    model reaches the same objective as FOCE/FOCEI/Laplace.
  - **A dose implied by `ADDL` takes covariates, `F`, `D{n}`, `TIME` and
    occasion from the record that ends its interval, and its lag from the row
    that carried `ADDL`, as in NONMEM**
    ([ferx-core #1909](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/1909),
    [#2333](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2333)). Predictions on
    `ADDL` trains with time-varying covariates change. The population
    fingerprint scheme moves from 6 to 7, so a fit saved by an earlier ferx
    loads with its stored fingerprint treated as absent.
  - **FREM models use the covariate sigma on covariate rows on every
    fixed-EBE gradient path**
    ([ferx-core #2023](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2023),
    [#2334](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2334)): `method =
    "gn"` / `"gn_hybrid"`, `optimizer = "trust_region"` and `covariance_method
    = "s"` / `"rsr"` read the PK error model there. Such FREM fits change and
    are slower (finite differences per subject).
  - **Gradient optimizers on `[event_model]` / `[binary_model]` models with
    random effects take the reconverged score**
    ([ferx-core #1778](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/1778),
    [#2439](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2439)) and no longer
    stop short of the optimum as converged; such fits take 1.5-2.5x as long.
  - **Convergence is not reported from a stop above the optimum**:
    an unprofiled FOCE / FOCEI fit with L-BFGS or SLSQP verifies a converged
    stop with a leg at a tight inner tolerance
    ([ferx-core #2462](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2462),
    [#2472](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2472)); a profiled
    L-BFGS / SLSQP verification leg that stopped on its step tolerance while
    still moving is followed by another
    ([ferx-core #2340](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2340),
    [#2465](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2465)); and a theta
    driven onto the internal `1e-10` log-packing floor while the objective
    still falls is restarted from its initial value
    ([ferx-core #2339](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2339),
    [#2460](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2460)). A fit that
    keeps improving past its last leg, or stays on the floor, is reported not
    converged with a warning. Fits can take noticeably longer.
  - **SAEM mixture fits move subjects between classes**
    ([ferx-core #2458](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2458),
    [#2467](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2467)): with a
    class-switched mu-referenced typical value (`if (MIXNUM == 1)
    TVCL1 * exp(ETA_CL) else TVCL2 * exp(ETA_CL)`) the class-switched values
    and mixing fractions land on the maximum-likelihood estimate (the NONMEM
    anchor's `TVCL2` 3.14 -> 2.82). Where SAEM still cannot move the class
    (IOV, `mu_referencing = false`, a negative lower bound on a class theta)
    the fit warns.
  - **`ferx_simulate()` on a `[mixture]` model draws each subject's class**
    ([ferx-core #2163](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2163),
    [#2429](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2429)) from its mixing
    probabilities, per replicate and per uncertainty draw, and simulates it
    under that class's Omega and Sigma; every subject used to be simulated as
    class 1. The same holds for adaptive dosing and the NPDE reference
    simulation. Seeded eta and epsilon draws are unchanged.
  - **Profiled covariance follow-ups**
    ([ferx-core #2418](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2418),
    [#2419](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2419),
    [#2420](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2420),
    [#2453](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2453)): asymptotic and
    SIR uncertainty draws keep a level held on its bound fixed; the covariance
    step reports the re-solved level estimates its standard errors are
    computed at, with a new warning `W_COV_PROFILED_LEVELS_RESOLVED`; and a
    held-layout re-solve that fails the EBE guard fails the covariance step,
    and `ferx_get_warnings()` gives that failure the guarded re-solve's
    advice, naming which re-solve it was. A profiled fit no longer names sparse subjects below
    `min_obs_for_convergence_check` in its finite-difference warnings
    ([ferx-core #2408](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2408)).
  - **The covariance step of an ODE model that finite-differences runs at
    `ode_reltol = 1e-6` / `ode_abstol = 1e-8`** when the fit ran looser
    ([ferx-core #520](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/520),
    [#2344](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2344)); at the default
    tolerances its standard errors could come out 2-7x too large with no
    warning. Standard errors move for ODE models on the finite-difference or
    salvaged route, and under `covariance_method = "s"`. A step that cannot
    tighten warns `W_COV_ODE_TOLERANCE_NOT_TIGHTENED`.
  - **Finite-difference gradients step a small theta relative to its size**
    ([ferx-core #2187](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2187),
    [#2398](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2398)): the reconverged
    outer gradient, the mixture gradient and the SAEM / VI theta differences
    stepped by an absolute `eps`, 15% off on a theta of 8e-4.
  - **A hand-written Box-Cox eta, or any `exp(eta)` under a `^`, `+` / `-` or
    function, no longer prints a log-normal `CV%`**
    ([ferx-core #1714](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/1714),
    [#1720](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/1720)); labels only.
  - **`inits_from_nca` no longer writes the volume onto `TVCL`**
    ([ferx-core #1922](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/1922),
    [#2400](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2400)) when `V = TVV`
    has no eta: the name fallback matches whole words only, and two roles
    that resolve to one theta are left at the model default with a warning.
  - **An `ode_solver` warning about a rejected analytic-sensitivity solve no
    longer recommends another `ode_method`**
    ([ferx-core #1347](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/1347),
    [#2475](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2475)), which its own
    text said would not help.
