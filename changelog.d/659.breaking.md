- **`ferx_covariance(fit)` replays the fit's own `covariance_method`** (#659): the
  argument now defaults to `NULL`, the method the fit asked for
  (`fit$call_settings`, then the model file's `[fit_options]`, then `"r"`), instead
  of `"r"`. A fit made with `covariance_method = "rsr"` or `"s"` used to be replayed
  as `"r"` unless the caller repeated it; on `mbma_placebo` the replayed standard
  errors sat 17-19% from the fit's. Pass `covariance_method = "r"` for the old
  behaviour; passed explicitly, `"r"` is now held to on large fits as it is in
  `ferx_fit()`, where a defaulted `"r"` on finite differences is routed onto `"s"`.
  The estimator a covariance step ran is recorded as `fit$covariance_method` (`NA`
  without a matrix), refreshed by `ferx_covariance()` and kept by `ferx_save_fit()` /
  `ferx_load_fit()`. A replay that runs another estimator than the recorded one
  warns, and a fit saved before this release, which records none, gets a message
  naming the method it is replayed under. `ferx_sir()` is unaffected: it takes the
  fit's covariance matrix as its proposal and re-derives no covariance step.
