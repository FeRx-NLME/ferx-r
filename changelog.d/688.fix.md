- `ferx_xpose()` now hands Xpose the Gaussian observation rows of `fit$sdtab`
  only. On a model with a binary or time-to-event endpoint, the discrete
  rows and the TTE residual rows (`COXSNELL`, `MARTINGALE`, `DEVIANCE`, `PIT`)
  that follow them used to be counted in `nobs`, plotted as DV values, and
  made the parameter / eta join fall back to matching by ID. They are now
  left out, with a message saying how many, and each row picks up its own
  subject's parameters from the `fit$subject_summary` counts. A fit with no
  Gaussian observations at all is refused with a clear error (#688).
