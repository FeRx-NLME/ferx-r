- `fit$sdtab` gains `EPRED`, `PWRES` and `EWRES` whenever NPDE is computed
  (`settings = list(npde_nsim = ...)` or `ferx_calc_npde()`), from the same
  simulation at no extra cost (#748, ferx-core #2871). `EPRED` is the Monte-Carlo
  expected prediction; `PWRES` (also written as NONMEM's `EWRES`) is the
  population-weighted residual `Sigma_sim^(-1/2) (y - EPRED)`, the
  simulation-based analogue of CWRES, decorrelated like NONMEM's default
  `EWRES`. `ferx_xpose()` maps `EPRED` to the prediction role and
  `PWRES`/`EWRES` to the residual role.
