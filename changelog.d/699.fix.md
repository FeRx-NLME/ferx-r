- **Engine fixes from ferx-core `bbda824e..aa5f3b6e`** (#699):

  - **A fit warm-started at a converged optimum reports converged**: a
    bootstrap replicate or a refit from a previous fit's estimates no longer
    carries `W_STALLED_AT_INIT` with `converged = FALSE`. The engine restarts
    an outer optimizer that did not move and reports the lower of the start
    and the restart; profiled fits are covered too
    ([ferx-core #2506](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2506),
    [#2591](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2591)).
  - **A fit with a `profile`d theta level block is held against its own
    start**: one that ends materially above its starting objective carries
    `W_ABOVE_START` and is no longer reported as converged, also when the
    first profile solve fails outright
    ([ferx-core #2519](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2519),
    [#2619](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2619)).
  - **`min_obs_for_convergence_check` counts time-to-event and binary
    records**, not only Gaussian observations, so the unconverged EBE of a
    non-Gaussian-only subject now counts toward `max_unconverged_frac`; such
    fits can report, and stop on, unconverged EBEs they used to hide.
    `W_COV_RSR_ONE_SUBJECT_LEVELS` likewise counts binary records
    ([ferx-core #2111](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2111)).
  - **`ferx_predict_survival()` returns each subject's rows in ascending
    `CMT` order on every call**, and multi-endpoint objectives no longer vary
    in their last bits between runs
    ([ferx-core #2531](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2531)).
  - **`total_ebe_fallbacks` counts the EBEs a fallback returned**, not every
    inner solve in which one ran; a solve is counted in at most one of it and
    `total_ebe_nm_polished`, and the count can only go down
    ([ferx-core #2589](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2589)).
