- **`parameter_scaling` / `scale_params` docs match the engine** (#634):
  `?ferx_fit` said `parameter_scaling = "auto"` applies `"rescale2"` to the
  gradient-based optimizers and recommended it for cold starts. The engine's
  `"auto"` applies `"abs"` to `nlopt_lbfgs` and `slsqp`, leaves `bobyqa`, `mma`
  and `trust_region` unscaled, and uses `"rescale2"` for none of them (it drove
  L-BFGS into a bound on warfarin FOCEI). The entries now also say that an
  identity-packed theta switches magnitude scaling off on the NLopt optimizers,
  and that `"abs"` differs from `scale_params = TRUE` by the near-zero
  identity-theta probe it alone runs (ferx-core #2159).
