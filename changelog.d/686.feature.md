- `ferx_simulate_adaptive()` gains `etas =` and `unmatched =` (ferx-core #1594,
  #2162). `etas` is a data frame with an `ID` column and one column per eta, in
  the model's order; `fit$ebe_etas` has that shape, so
  `ferx_simulate_adaptive(model, fit = fit, etas = fit$ebe_etas)` runs each
  fitted subject at its EBEs instead of a draw from omega. Kappas (drawn per
  decision window) and the assay error are still drawn. `unmatched = "refuse"`
  (the default) refuses a data subject without a row (`E_SUPPLIED_ETAS`, naming
  `unmatched = "draw"` as the way out); `"draw"` draws that
  subject's eta and leaves the supplied subjects on their rows, and warns when
  no row matched any subject (IDs keyed differently, `01` against `1`). Wrong or
  misordered eta names, a repeated ID, a non-finite value and a `[mixture]` model
  are refused with `E_SUPPLIED_ETAS`. `$metrics` gains `ETA_SUPPLIED`. Works with
  and without `fit` (#686).
