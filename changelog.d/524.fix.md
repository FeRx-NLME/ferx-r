- **`ferx_load_fit()` loads a bundle whose `iov_occasion` it does not
  recognise** ([#524](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-r/issues/524)). A value
  other than `"column"`, `"per_dose"` or `{"time_windows": [...]}` - a newer
  ferx-core's variant, or a hand-edited key - used to stop the whole load. The
  field is now dropped with a `ferx_iov_occasion_dropped_warning` naming the
  value, `fit$iov_occasion` is `NULL`, and `ferx_sir()` / `ferx_covariance()`
  fall back to the model file's rule, as for a bundle saved before ferx-core
  #1783.
