- **`ferx_sir()` / `ferx_covariance()` say to re-fit a text-ID fit that
  predates `fit$subject_ids`** (#555): on such a fit with no random effects and
  no `[individual_parameters]` (e.g. kept with `saveRDS()`), the IDs come from
  `fit$sdtab$ID`, which holds a text ID's position, and the engine refused the
  population as not the fit's. The refusal now adds that cause and the advice
  to re-fit with `ferx_fit(model, data)`; the engine's message and code are
  unchanged.
