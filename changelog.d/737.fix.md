- **Engine fixes from ferx-core `3cd15863..7c63c7a5`** (#737):

  - **The positive Durbin-Watson warning on a compartment-free model** (an MBMA
    or other algebraic `[structural_model]`) no longer recommends a transit
    absorption model, an extra compartment or IOV on ka/F, none of which such a
    model has. It names a `correlation ar1(RHO) by ...` line instead, and
    `ferx_get_warnings()` no longer appends the compartment advice under it
    ([ferx-core #2798](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2798)).
  - An M3-censored row whose limit is astronomically far on the certain side
    (an LLOQ of `1e300` on a `CENS=1` row) no longer sends the objective to the
    divergence sentinel. The row now scores as the certain observation it is
    ([ferx-core #2762](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2762),
    [#2772](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2772)).
  - The analytic inverse-Gaussian absorption (`one_cpt_ig` / `two_cpt_ig`, and
    an infused dose into an `igd()` input) is correct for a narrow
    absorption-time distribution. Below a CV of about 5% its predictions were
    wrong (about `1e48` at CV 0.05, infinite from 0.03 down)
    ([ferx-core #2769](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2769)).
  - A `.fitrx` bundle with a `held_at_bound` reason that a later ferx added now
    loads: the reason reads as `"unknown"` and its SE still shows as `N/A`
    ([ferx-core #2817](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2817)).
  - `ferx_covsearch()` no longer loses its backward phase on a model that reads
    `D1` by name for `RATE=-2` doses. A backward phase that ends with effects it
    could not test now says so
    ([ferx-core #2789](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2789)).
  - On data whose only records are time-to-event, count or CTMM state records,
    a `theta NAME[COL]` level block with no levels is no longer refused as "the
    data carries no observation rows". `E_THETA_LEVEL_BINDING` names the record
    kinds the data carries and ends on a next step
    ([ferx-core #2751](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2751),
    [#2808](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2808),
    [#2819](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2819)).
