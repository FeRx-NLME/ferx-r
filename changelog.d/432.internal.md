- **The `simulation_warnings` attribute can only carry a ferx-core
  diagnostics bundle** ([#432](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-r/issues/432)).
  The glue helper that attaches it takes the engine's `*_diag` result itself
  (through a `CoreDiag` trait on `SimulationOutput`, `PredictionOutput`,
  `SurvivalPredictionOutput` and `AdaptiveSimulationResult`) rather than a
  plain character vector, so relaying the data reader's raw warnings - the
  #426 regression - no longer compiles. No change to what any call returns.
