- **`selection = "fit"` reads the data as the fit read them** (#793):
  `ferx_simulate()`, `ferx_simulate_with_uncertainty()`, `ferx_predict()`,
  `ferx_predict_survival()`, `ferx_vpc_tte()`, `ferx_vpc_kmmc()` and
  `ferx_vpc_cif()` take `selection = c("all", "fit")` when given a `fit`.
  `"fit"` applies the fit's own data selection (the model file's
  `[data_selection]` and any `ferx_fit(ignore =, accept =, ignore_ids =)`) to
  every record it removed, doses included: a simulation or prediction then has
  `fit$sdtab`'s observation rows in order, and a survival prediction or a TTE
  VPC (its observed curve and its replicates) covers the fit's subjects. On the
  fit's own data the rows are checked against `fit$population_fingerprint`.
  `"all"`, the default, reads every record as before. For one seed the two are
  different simulations, not one and a filter of it.
