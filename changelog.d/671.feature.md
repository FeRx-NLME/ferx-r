- **`ferx_simulate_with_uncertainty()` simulates a `[mixture]` fit with
  per-class overrides** (#656, #671;
  [ferx-core #2163](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2163),
  [#2429](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2429)). It used to
  refuse such a fit with `E_MIXTURE_SIMULATION`. Each subject now draws its
  class from the mixing probabilities in every `DRAW` and `SIM`, and runs
  under that class's Omega and Sigma, drawn with the rest of the parameters:
  an `omega(k)` / `sigma(k)` override is drawn around its fitted value in
  `fit$mixture_overrides`, and a FIX one is held there.
