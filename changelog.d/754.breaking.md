- **`ferx_vpc_tte()` reads the observed data on `[0, horizon]` only** (#754,
  [ferx-core #2950](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2950)): with
  a `horizon` below the data's follow-up, a record after it is a censoring at
  the horizon. The curve and its band up to the horizon do not change, but
  `$observed` now ends at the horizon instead of running past the band, and
  the observed value (`$band$obs`) at a `grid` time past the horizon is `NA`.
  With `horizon = NULL` nothing changes. On a repeated-events endpoint,
  `ferx_vpc_tte()` used to refuse with `E_VPC_TTE_INPUT`; it now returns the
  RTTE VPC.
