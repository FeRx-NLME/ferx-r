- `progress` on `ferx_bootstrap()` and on every search (`ferx_covsearch()`,
  `ferx_iivsearch()`, `ferx_iovsearch()`, `ferx_ruvsearch()`,
  `ferx_modelsearch()`, `ferx_globalsearch()`, `ferx_amd()`) now also takes
  `"lines"`: plain progress on stdout, one record per line, flushed as each fit
  finishes, so a script's log or a job card shows it live. The bootstrap prints
  a line per replicate (sample index, OFV, `converged` / `not_converged` /
  `stalled` / `failed`, and the exclusion filter that skips it); a search
  prints its per-step summary plus a line per candidate (step, id, features,
  OFV, criterion value, `passed` or `failed (<gate>)`). `progress = "bar"`
  names the interactive display (#677).
