- **Line-oriented progress for scripts** (#677): `progress` on
  `ferx_bootstrap()` and on every search (`ferx_covsearch()`,
  `ferx_iivsearch()`, `ferx_ruvsearch()`, `ferx_iovsearch()`,
  `ferx_modelsearch()`, `ferx_globalsearch()`, `ferx_amd()`) now also takes
  `"lines"`: plain records on stdout, one per line and flushed as they happen,
  so a log viewer tailing a job's output sees the run move. `progress = TRUE`
  picks it on its own in a non-interactive session (`Rscript`), where it used
  to draw the bootstrap's bar or write the searches' step messages to stderr;
  interactive sessions keep those. The bootstrap prints one line per replicate
  as it finishes, with its sample index, OFV and outcome (converged,
  minimization terminated, failed, and whether the `skip_*` filters exclude
  it; reused replicates are listed too); the searches print one line per
  candidate as its fit finishes, with its id, features, OFV, criterion and
  verdict (passed, or the gates it failed); a candidate deduplicated against
  another in its batch prints as `duplicate of <id>`. Both are read from the run's
  journal, so a run without a `directory` journals into a temporary one.
