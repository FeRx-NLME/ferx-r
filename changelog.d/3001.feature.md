- **`settings = list(mstep_optimizer = ...)` picks the SAEM / IMP / IMPMAP
  numerical M-step optimizer** (ferx-core
  [#3001](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/3001), ferx-core pin
  moved to `8391287e`). `"auto"` (the default) uses Fisher scoring on the exact
  adjoint score (`"newton"`) where the model is in its scope and `"bobyqa"`
  otherwise; `"bobyqa"` and `"lbfgs"` force those solvers. `fit$mstep_optimizer_used`
  says what `"auto"` resolved to (`NULL` where no numerical M-step runs). SAEM,
  IMP and IMPMAP estimates and OFVs move at the default: the M-step solves to
  its tolerance rather than a fixed evaluation budget, and ferx-core #3021
  also changes seeded SAEM / IMP draws. `mixture_gradient_fd` was removed from
  ferx-core (#3009); ferx-r did not use it.
