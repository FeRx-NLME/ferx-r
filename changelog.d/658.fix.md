- **`ferx_covariance()` replays the fit's own covariance-step settings**
  (#658): `fd_hessian_step`, `analytic_cov_hessian` and `cov_inner_tol`, as
  `ferx_fit()` was given them (`fit$call_settings`) or the model file's
  `[fit_options]` set them (`fit$model_file_settings`), the call first. The
  engine does not record these, and the replay used its defaults whatever the
  fit had used, so `ferx_covariance(fit)` was not the fit's own step: on
  warfarin with finite differences and `fd_hessian_step = 0.1` its standard
  errors sat up to 6.5% from the fit's. A profiled fit made with
  `fd_hessian_step = 0.01` would have been replayed at the new profiled
  default `3e-3`. The replay is now `identical()` to the fit's step, also
  after `ferx_save_fit()` / `ferx_load_fit()`. A bundle written by ferx-core,
  which carries no R settings, still gets the engine defaults.
