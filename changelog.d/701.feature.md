- **Engine features from ferx-core `aa5f3b6e..86a05bd5`** (#701):

  - **Opt-in f-SAEM E-step kernel, `settings = list(saem_kernel = "imh")`**
    ([ferx-core #1449](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/1449),
    [#2639](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2639)): each
    subject's block random-walk proposals become draws from a Laplace proposal
    centred on its conditional mode, with a small defensive prior component.
    It runs on analytic models and on ODE models the dual walk covers, and
    declines to the random walk, with a warning, elsewhere and on data with
    fewer than 2 observations per subject per random effect. New settings
    `saem_imh_proposals`, `saem_imh_refresh`, `saem_imh_defensive` and
    `saem_imh_min_obs_per_eta`. The default `saem_kernel = "rw"` is unchanged.
  - **The mid-leg basin probe no longer cuts every leg of a large fit short**
    ([ferx-core #2600](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2600)):
    a probe now restarts only when at least 1 % of the subjects switched mode,
    so a 5,937-subject FOCEI fit is no longer stopped at evaluation 32 of each
    leg and left `converged = FALSE`.
