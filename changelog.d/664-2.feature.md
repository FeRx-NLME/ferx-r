- **`settings = list(inner_twin_seed = )`** (#664): the engine's flip-flop twin
  seed for the inner multi-start (ferx-core #2425, on by default for closed-form
  `one_cpt_oral` models) is documented in `?ferx_fit` and recorded in
  `fit$scoring_settings`. A fit saved before it was recorded reads it as
  `FALSE`, so `ferx_covariance()` and `ferx_sir()` re-score that fit without a
  twin seed, as it was fitted.
