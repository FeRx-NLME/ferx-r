- **`ferx_covariance()` and `ferx_sir()` classify their replay's warnings the
  way `ferx_fit()` does** (#611): the engine's warnings from a post-hoc
  covariance or SIR run are classified by ferx-core's `classify_warning`
  instead of being stamped `warning` / `covariance` (or `sir`). A replayed
  "Covariance step failed ..." is now the same `critical` / `covariance_failed`
  row as the fit's own would be, so it counts as critical in the warning tally
  and shows `(covariance_failed)` on `print()`'s STATUS line. That is new
  wherever the fit's own step did not fail - it was off, or it succeeded where
  the replay does not - which used to show the replay's failure as a plain
  warning. The replay's covariance warnings also replace the fit's own, so a
  fit whose step failed no longer reports `covariance_failed` beside the matrix
  a successful `ferx_covariance()` just computed, and a failure is no longer
  listed twice under two classes. `check_strictness()` is unaffected: it does
  not read severity. The `SIR: ` messages were already classed `sir` /
  `warning` and are unchanged; the step's other notes (such as the stale
  population-fingerprint note) now take their engine class, usually `general`.
