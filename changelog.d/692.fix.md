- **Engine fixes from ferx-core `0703293c..497f918b`** (#692):

  - **A failed event-time solve no longer aborts a drug-driven TTE
    simulation**
    ([ferx-core #2087](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2087),
    [#2566](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2566)): the subject
    whose ODE hazard cannot be solved is censored at the observation window
    and named in a `W_TTE_SOLVE_FAILED` warning; the rest of the population
    still simulates. From R this was an error.
  - **Competing-risks TTE simulation with a fixed seed is reproducible across
    runs**
    ([ferx-core #2569](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2569),
    [#2572](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2572)).
  - **An ETA or kappa that enters its scale twice no longer prints a CV% or
    SD** (`TVV * exp(ETA_V) * exp(ETA_V)` is `2 * eta` on its scale); the row
    is labelled `custom`
    ([ferx-core #1724](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/1724),
    [#2514](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2514)).
  - **`ferx_trace()`'s `method` column names the objective a fit scores**:
    `"laplace"` at one quadrature node, `"agq"` with `n_agq > 1`, where a
    Laplace fit was traced `"foce"` and an AGQ fit `"focei"` or `"laplace"`
    ([ferx-core #2552](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2552),
    [#2560](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2560)).
  - **`inner_optimizer = "nelder_mead"` reports a subject converged only at
    its mode**; fits under this option can move slightly, to the stationary
    EBEs
    ([ferx-core #2557](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2557),
    [#2564](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2564)).
  - **A profiled fit near its optimum keeps the exact level gradient**, so
    `W_PROFILE_FD_GRADIENT` fires less
    ([ferx-core #2539](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2539),
    [#2554](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2554)).
  - **A FOCE/FOCEI verification leg starts at the incumbent's EBEs**, so an
    ODE fit no longer reports a point far above the optimum it had reached
    after a cold leg was rejected
    ([ferx-core #2549](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2549),
    [#2551](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2551)).
