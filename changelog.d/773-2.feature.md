- **New with ferx-core `13e6c8bf..00b5c313`** (#773):

  - The inner (EBE) stall escape now runs by default on an ODE model whose
    prediction has a kink (an `if`, `min`, `max`, `clamp`, `abs`, `floor`,
    `ceil` or `round` reading a random effect or a state), so an EBE no longer
    stops on a kink's valley floor without `settings = list(inner_kick = TRUE)`.
    Such a fit can move, and can now end with `W_EBE_START_DEPENDENT`; a model
    without a kink is unchanged, bit for bit
    ([ferx-core #3210](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/3210)).
  - Per-subject passes balance better across few threads: FOCEI on a
    1,518-subject ODE+IOV model on 2 threads went from 277 s to 210 s, with
    unchanged results
    ([ferx-core #3216](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/3216)).
