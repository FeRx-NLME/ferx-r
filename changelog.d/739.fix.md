- **A `[mixture]` per-class override collapsed onto the internal guard is held**
  (#739): an `omega(k)` / `sigma(k)` override that collapses onto the internal
  lower guard is now held at the floor like a FIX parameter and listed in
  `fit$held_at_bound`, as `<base>_MIX<k>` with its class in the new `class`
  column (`NA` for a base parameter). The base parameter keeps its own SE, and
  the other SEs and SIR intervals equal a fit with the override FIX
  ([ferx-core #2809](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2809)).
