- **`ferx_covariance()` holds a theta the engine froze during the fit** (#613):
  a theta with no effect on the objective is frozen at its initial value
  (the `flat_parameter` warning) and recorded in `fit$theta_fixed`, not in the
  model file. The replay rebuilt the FIX mask from the file, differentiated the
  frozen theta and failed on its zero diagonal, so a fit whose own step computed
  printed `(covariance_failed)` afterwards. It now holds the fit's
  `theta_fixed`, reproducing the in-fit step (the frozen theta's SE is 0);
  `ferx_sir()` does the same, and `fit$cov_fixed` marks such a theta as held.
