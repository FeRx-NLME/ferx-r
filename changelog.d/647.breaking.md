- **ferx builds against ferx-core `59a4fe3f`, up from `0cbad3c2`**, since
  #622, #630 and #635, which moved the pin without announcing these engine
  changes (#647). ferx-core #2173, #2156 and #2217 from the same range are in
  the `5bd862b8` entry; #2038, #2164, #2225 and #2231 were announced with the
  R changes that use them. Numbers compare ferx-r at the two pins (macOS
  arm64). These engine changes reach R:

  - **A negative absorption lag at the initial estimates is a fit error**
    ([ferx-core #1189](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/1189)).
    A `lagtime` / `ALAG` or per-route `lag=` that is below 0 at the typical
    values makes `ferx_fit()` stop with `E_NEGATIVE_LAGTIME` on `e$code`,
    naming the subject and dose, as NONMEM does. It used to warn and fit
    anyway: warfarin with `lagtime = TVLAG` started at -0.2 fitted to OFV
    -287.63, its doses' input starting before their records. `ferx_predict()`
    and `ferx_simulate()` still serve a negative lag, with a warning.
  - **An analytic hazard that reads `TIME` is refused when the model is
    parsed**
    ([ferx-core #1919](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/1919)).
    A `scale`, `shape`, `alpha`, `gamma` or `loghr` in `[event_model]` that
    reads `TIME`, directly or through an `[individual_parameters]` value, was
    evaluated at `TIME = 0`, dropping the time term: `tte_weibull` with
    `scale = TVSCALE * exp(ETA_SCALE) * (1 + 0.01 * TIME)` fitted to OFV
    222.29. It is now an `E_PARSE` error naming the parameter. Write a
    time-varying hazard as an ODE-accumulated `hazard = ...` expression.
  - **A theta with a negative lower bound started close to zero is
    estimated**
    ([ferx-core #2159](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2159)).
    Before the NLopt optimizers start, such a theta with |initial| < 0.1 is
    probed, and divided by its initial magnitude when the objective sees it.
    Warfarin FOCEI with `V = TVV_S / 1e-6` and `TVV_S` started at 1e-5 used
    to stop at OFV -205.97 under `optimizer = "bobyqa"`, -198.95 under
    `"slsqp"` and -269.47 under `"lbfgs"`, each with `converged = TRUE`. All
    three now reach -286.0042, the plain model's optimum. Applies under the
    default `parameter_scaling = "auto"` and under `"abs"`.
  - **Covariance-step finite differences no longer step a small such theta
    through zero**
    ([ferx-core #2147](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2147)).
    A theta with a negative lower bound and a non-zero initial value below 1
    in magnitude is now stepped relative to that value, on the R-matrix
    stencil (`analytic_cov_hessian = FALSE`, and every model outside the
    analytic R-matrix) and in the per-subject scores behind
    `covariance_method = "s"` / `"rsr"`. Warfarin with
    `V = TVV_S / 1e-3` (`TVV_S` = 0.0077 at the optimum) and
    `covariance_method = "r"` reported SE(TVCL) 2.27e-6; it now reports
    0.00710, the same as with `V = TVV_S`. Standard errors move on such
    models whatever the theta's size: `pktte_joint`, whose `TVBETA` starts
    at 0.5, has SE(TVCL) 2.038 (was 1.973) at the same estimates.
  - **A random effect or theta inside `a mod b` is estimated**
    ([ferx-core #2150](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2150)).
    The derivative of `mod` was taken as 0, so the inner optimizer never
    moved an eta read through it: warfarin with
    `V = TVV * exp((ETA_V + 5) mod 10 - 5)` stopped at OFV -78.23 with
    `omega(ETA_V)` 0.98. It now fits as the plain model does (-286.0042,
    0.0096). `x mod 0` is also 0 in every block, as it was in
    `[individual_parameters]`; `[derived]`, hazard, Markov and mixture-weight
    expressions returned `NaN`
    ([#2149](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2149)).
  - **Bundled examples**: 64 of the 70 example fits keep their OFV to 1e-6.
    `warfarin_dcm` stops at -1185.46, where it reached -1190.33: the run no
    longer detects a trapped warm start and restarts from it, and its
    "minimised against empirical Bayes estimates" warning is gone.
    `warfarin_sde` ends at -280.51 (was -280.57), still `converged = FALSE`
    with `W_EBE_START_DEPENDENT`. `biphasic_igd_absorption`,
    `two_cpt_oral_base`, `two_cpt_oral_global` and `warfarin_logit_f` move by
    less than 0.002. `warfarin_ode_time` no longer falls back to fixed-EBE
    outer gradients for one subject. Changes in the range behind these:
    - an estimating fit's first evaluation starts from the EBEs the
      flat-theta pre-flight already found, instead of solving them cold
      again; the trajectory after it can differ at the level of optimizer
      noise ([#2076](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2076));
    - the dense-BFGS inner solve (the IOV inner solve among them) bounds its
      steps after a non-descent reset, which used to send a steep start to
      the Nelder-Mead fallback
      ([#2099](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2099));
    - the exact gradient takes the right value for an `else if` arm under an
      earlier eta- or theta-dependent condition
      ([#1927](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/1927)), and for
      an ODE infusion or `zero_order` window that ends a few ULPs before a
      covariate-changing record
      ([#1160](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/1160)).
  - **Predictions**: a transit or inverse-Gaussian absorption model whose
    `CL`, `V` or absorption parameter is `NaN` is served `NaN` from that
    record on, and the fit reports `W_NONFINITE_OBJECTIVE`; it was served
    0 and scored with a finite objective
    ([#2134](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2134)). ODE
    compartment states (sdtab state columns, `[derived]`) read an observation
    listed before a co-timed dose at the subject's first time pre-dose, as
    IPRED does ([#2139](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2139)).
  - **Covariates bind by exact case** with a `[covariates]` block: a model
    reading `wt` no longer binds a `WT` column; it stays unread and is
    reported
    ([#1946](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/1946)).
  - **Warnings**:
    - `method = "gn_hybrid"` reports the warnings of its FOCEI polish when the
      polish's estimates are the ones reported, such as
      `W_EBE_START_DEPENDENT` and an unconverged outer optimizer
      ([#2098](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2098)).
    - A `[binary_model]` endpoint's EBE search uses the exact gradient, and
      the "N of M subjects use finite-difference inner gradients" warning
      counts the route each subject takes: `pktte_joint` now warns for its 6
      subjects, whose time-to-event term runs on finite differences
      ([#2024](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2024)).
    - `fit$gradient_method_inner` and `print(fit)` read `"analytic (Dual2)"`
      for a residual `weight =` with `block_sigma` or `correlation`, where the
      search already was analytic; non-IOV subjects of such a model now
      search on another analytic kernel, and their EBEs move at round-off
      ([#2209](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2209)).
    - The data reader's missing-covariate warning is `W_COVARIATE_MISSING`,
      category `data_quality`
      ([#2041](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2041)).
    - A fit with `npde_nsim` counts reference replicates clamped by the
      flip-flop guard of a transit or inverse-Gaussian closed form in
      `W_SIMULATED_FLIP_FLOP`
      ([#1928](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/1928)).
    - The covariance step's eigenvalue-floor warning prints the
      `|min eig|/max eig` ratio only when the Hessian is indefinite
      ([#2132](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2132)); the
      pre-fit cost note and `fit$covariance_n_evals_estimated` count only the
      subjects outside the analytic R-matrix
      ([#2054](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2054)); and
      `covariance_method = "rsr"` above 100 free parameters warns of its cost
      as `"r"` does
      ([#2055](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2055)).
  - **Faster**: linear `[odes]` solved by the matrix exponential skip
    structural zeros; results are unchanged, but a fit may take a different
    number of steps to the same optimum
    ([#2210](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2210)).
