- **Engine features from ferx-core `b259dd10..3f87757e`** (#681):

  - **`block_sigma` now works together with inter-occasion variability
    (`kappa`)** under `method = "foce"` and `"focei"`
    ([ferx-core #979](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/979),
    [#2478](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2478)). Such a model,
    for example total and unbound concentrations with correlated residuals and
    a kappa on CL, used to be refused with `E_BLOCK_SIGMA_IOV_UNSUPPORTED`. On
    a two-endpoint NONMEM anchor the objective matches to 5e-8. Other
    estimators (`saem`, `imp`, `laplace`, `vi`, `n_agq > 1`) still refuse the
    combination.
  - **A binary endpoint with IOV is accepted**
    ([ferx-core #2482](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2482)); it
    was refused with `E_BINARY_IOV_UNSUPPORTED`.
  - **Exact derivatives replace the remaining finite differences**
    ([ferx-core #2482](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2482)):
    time-to-event, binary and CTMM endpoints, drug-driven CTMM generators,
    hazards reading `TIME`, steady-state rows of joint PK-TTE models and
    mixtures with IOV or a block omega now have exact gradients in the fit,
    the covariance step and the SAEM/IMP M-step. With a gradient optimizer
    time-to-event fits run 7-14x faster and binary, CTMM and joint PK-TTE fits
    1.5-2x faster. Only SDE models, `gradient = "fd"` and out-of-scope
    subjects still use finite differences.
  - **ODE models with IOV fit in about 4x fewer outer evaluations under
    FOCE/FOCEI again**
    ([ferx-core #2508](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2508),
    [#2521](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2521)): the outer
    loop restarts kappa at 0 on ODE + IOV models instead of carrying the
    previous evaluation's estimate, which had made the objective depend on the
    optimizer's path. OFVs can move in the integration-error digits.
  - **On a fit with a `profile`d level block, the warning for subjects whose
    level gradient fell back to finite differences now starts with
    `W_PROFILE_FD_GRADIENT`** and is classified `gradient_fallback` (info) by
    that code; with `verbose = TRUE` the fit ends with a line summing its
    profile work (Newton steps, EBE re-solves, adjoint vs finite-difference
    level gradients)
    ([ferx-core #2405](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2405),
    [#2493](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2493)).
  - **A `[covariate_nn]` with no `init` is warned about**
    (`W_NN_BARE_OUTPUT_START`): every output then starts at `softplus(0)`
    whatever parameter it feeds, a start from which a two-compartment DCM fit
    reported convergence far from the optimum
    ([ferx-core #1561](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/1561),
    [#2479](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2479)).
  - **`ferx_simulate_with_uncertainty()` checks the model and data once per
    run, not once per draw**
    ([ferx-core #1288](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/1288),
    [#2503](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2503)).
