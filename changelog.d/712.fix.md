- **`ferx_covariance()`, `ferx_sir()` and `ferx_calc_npde()` return a new fit
  with new bytes** (#712): each runs on the fit's bundle and leaves its input
  unaltered, in place of grafting fields onto the list, so a fit's list and its
  saved bundle agree in any order of save, load and post-hoc step. The steps now
  see the whole fit, and `ferx_covariance()` keeps a fit's SIR draws and
  `ferx_calc_npde()`'s columns survive `ferx_save_fit()`. A background job that
  did not ask for a trace leaves none in its bundle either (`ferx_collect()`).
  A failed `ferx_save_fit()` leaves what was at the path.
