- **ferx-core pin moved to `a8f5f056`** (#761). Engine changes that reach R:

  - `[mixture]` models fit under `method = "laplace"` and `"focei"` with
    `n_agq > 1`, with the exact outer gradient; they used to be refused
    (ferx-core #3025).
  - `optimizer = "auto"` on a `[mixture]` model now picks L-BFGS on the exact
    mixture gradient, as for a single population, instead of BOBYQA, so mixture
    estimates and OFVs can move. A fit started with nearly identical classes can
    stop at a merged-class saddle (`W_MIXTURE_MERGED_CLASSES`): start the classes
    apart, or ask for `optimizer = "bobyqa"`. `gradient = "fd"` on a mixture now
    really differentiates by finite differences (ferx-core #3025).
  - A fit of an ODE model re-scores its OFV once at a tenfold tighter
    `ode_reltol` / `ode_abstol` and warns with `W_ODE_OFV_NOT_SETTLED` when it
    moves by more than 0.1, or with `W_ODE_OFV_SETTLEMENT_UNCHECKED` when the
    check could not be completed (category `ode_solver`). `ferx_get_warnings()`
    gives each its own advice instead of the solver-setting advice for unclean
    integration. Estimates and OFV are unchanged (ferx-core #2994).
  - An ODE fit whose rejected trial step went through a region with no real
    right-hand side (e.g. a Hill term with a negative concentration) keeps its
    analytic gradient instead of falling back to finite differences on every
    evaluation; such fits can now converge (ferx-core #3065).
  - Less memory and time for ODE fits on the analytic gradient, correlated
    residuals and IOV EBE searches, with bit-identical results (ferx-core
    #3060, #3076, #3077, #3078, #3079, #3090).
