- A `ferx_fit` is now read-only: assigning to a core field (`fit$theta <- ...`,
  `fit[["theta"]] <- ...`, `fit["theta"] <- ...`) is an error of class
  `ferx_fit_readonly_error`, and `ferx_save_fit()`, `ferx_covariance()`,
  `ferx_sir()` and `ferx_calc_npde()` refuse a fit whose core fields no longer
  match the digest taken when it was built, such as one edited through
  `unclass()` (`ferx_fit_modified_error`). `ferx_save_fit()` also refuses a fit
  with no digest, including one read from an `.rds` written before this version:
  refit it. Your own objects can still be added under a new name
  (`fit$reviewer_note <- "final run"`); they keep through those steps and survive
  `ferx_save_fit()` / `ferx_load_fit()` in the bundle's `interface_extras.json`,
  and one that cannot be written as JSON, such as a function, is left out with a
  `ferx_fit_addition_dropped_warning` (#713).
