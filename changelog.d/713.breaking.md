- **Fits are read-only** (#713). Assigning to a field of a fit
  (`fit$theta <- ...`, `fit[["sdtab"]] <- ...`, `fit["omega"] <- ...`, also
  `fit$x <- NULL`) is an error of class `ferx_fit_read_only_error`: the list is
  a view of the fit's `.fitrx` bytes (`fit$bundle`), and an edit never reached
  them. A changed fit comes from `ferx_covariance()`, `ferx_sir()`,
  `ferx_calc_npde()` or a new `ferx_fit()`. `fit$model_path` and
  `fit$data_path`, which say where the files are now, may still be set.
  `ferx_save_fit()` refuses, with a `ferx_fit_edited_error` naming the fields,
  a fit whose fields were changed around the guards (`unclass()`, edit,
  reclass) or that carries no record of them (one built by hand, or kept in an
  `.rds` by an earlier development version; reload or refit it).
