- **`ferx_vpc()`: visual predictive check and pcVPC of a fit's continuous
  observations** (#787). `ferx_vpc(fit)` simulates `n_sim` replicates at the
  fit's estimates and hands them, with the fit's own observations, to
  `vpc::vpc()`; `pred_corr = TRUE` gives the prediction-corrected VPC. Each
  replicate is matched to the observations by compartment, subject, time and
  occurrence, so records a `[data_selection]` block ignored are not compared,
  every simulated row carries its observation's `PRED`, and `CENS = 1` / `-1`
  observations are blanked, with `lloq` / `uloq` taken from them (vpc takes
  one limit, so a fit censored on both sides is refused).
  `cmt =` picks one of several continuous endpoints, `stratify =` takes data
  columns (`"CMT"` shows every continuous endpoint), `horizon =` lets a joint
  PK-TTE model simulate, and `...` goes to `vpc::vpc()`. A pcVPC with a
  quantification limit is refused, since vpc 1.2.4 fails on it.
