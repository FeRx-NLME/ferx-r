- **Results and output that change with ferx-core `3cd15863..7c63c7a5`** (#737):

  - **The `STATUS` line of `print()` is the engine's verdict.** It reads
    `CONVERGED`, `CONVERGED WITH ISSUES` or `NOT CONVERGED`, followed by the
    reasons in a fixed order (`evaluation_only`, `budget_spent`, each critical
    warning's category, `covariance_failed`, `collapsed_at_guard: ETA_KA`).
    Before, it showed `converged` and the categories of every critical
    warning. A fit whose covariance step failed, or that collapsed a variance
    onto the guard without the step holding it, now prints
    `CONVERGED WITH ISSUES` where it printed `CONVERGED`. `fit$converged` is
    unchanged, apart from the stationarity check below
    ([ferx-core #2799](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2799)).
  - **A parameter collapsed onto ferx's internal lower guard no longer gets a
    standard error.** A variance or sigma at the floor of its log packing, or a
    theta with no declared lower bound on the internal `1e-10` floor, shows SE
    `N/A` in `print()` and `ferx_runlog()` and `NA` in `fit$estimates`. When
    the covariance step's `R` is clean, the step holds it like a `FIX`
    parameter (`fit$cov_fixed` marks it, its `fit$se_*` entry is `0`), and the
    other standard errors are conditional on it. They move by about 1e-5
    relative on ferx-core's reference fixture
    ([ferx-core #2797](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2797)).
  - **`converged = TRUE` now also requires a stationary point** for FOCE,
    FOCEI, Laplace / AGQ and Gauss-Newton fits. When the Newton decrement at the
    estimate is above 1 OFV unit, the fit reports `converged = FALSE` with a
    `W_NON_STATIONARY_OPTIMUM` warning (category `convergence`) naming the
    coordinates with the largest Newton steps, whatever the optimizer's own
    stop said. This check runs only when the fit has an unfloored covariance
    `R` matrix
    ([ferx-core #2785](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2785)).
  - **Durbin-Watson values move on every model.** `fit$dw_statistic` and
    `fit$iwres_lag1_r` now pair only consecutive rows of one subject that share
    an occasion (an IOV column, or a nested model's innermost level cell) and,
    under a `correlation ... by` line, a residual block. The Durbin-Watson
    denominator is pair-normalised, so independent residuals give 2 however
    short the series. The classic form read about `2 * pairs / rows` (1.70 on
    the naproxen MBMA). Values from earlier versions are not comparable; the
    1.5 / 2.5 thresholds are unchanged
    ([ferx-core #2798](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2798)).
  - **`RAYON_NUM_THREADS` now sets the worker count of a fit called without
    `threads`**, as given (no "minus one", no cap at 8), when the model file
    pins no `threads` either. An environment that sets `RAYON_NUM_THREADS=1`,
    such as many CI runners, now fits on one worker. An explicit `threads`
    still wins. A value that is not a non-negative integer is ignored with a
    warning
    ([ferx-core #2800](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2800)).
  - `method = "gn"` estimates change on models with a parameter on its bound:
    such a parameter whose gradient points out of the box is held for the
    iteration instead of collapsing the trust region. `one_cpt_transit`
    under GN went from OFV 1513.94 (trust radius collapsed) to 1492.17
    ([ferx-core #2777](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2777)).
  - A standalone `imp_eval_only` / `agq_eval_only` evaluation reports
    `covariance_status = "not_requested"` instead of `"failed"`, since it runs
    no covariance step. The multi-start "k of n starts failed" note is now an
    `info` warning, so it no longer makes a healthy returned fit look failed
    ([ferx-core #2799](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2799)).
