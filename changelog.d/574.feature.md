- **Sobol quasi-random importance sampling works with every proposal, and
  `imp` gets it too**
  ([#574](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-r/issues/574),
  [ferx-core #1965](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/1965)).
  `settings = list(impmap_sobol = TRUE)` now applies to the default
  Student-t proposal and combines with `imp_defensive_alpha > 0`, where it
  used to be ignored with a warning; under Sobol the defensive fraction is
  stratified. The new `imp_sobol` setting is the same switch for the
  estimating `imp` method (`imp_eval_only` stays pseudo-random). Under
  Sobol, `impmap_proposal_df` / `imp_proposal_df` must be at most 1000 (use
  `"normal"` beyond that). Sobol points are now Owen-scrambled instead of
  shifted (Cranley-Patterson), so seeded fits with `impmap_sobol = TRUE`
  change; fits without Sobol do not. Both settings stay off by default:
  they pay off with a fixed, small sample count (e.g. `impmap_auto = FALSE`,
  `impmap_samples` of 100-300), not under the adaptive default.
