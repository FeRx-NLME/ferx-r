- **Objects you add to a fit are saved with it** (#713). A new name
  (`fit$notes <- "reviewed"`, a data frame, a list) is yours to set, change
  and remove; `ferx_save_fit()` writes it into the bundle's
  `interface_extras.json` and `ferx_load_fit()` restores it, and
  `ferx_covariance()`, `ferx_sir()` and `ferx_calc_npde()` carry it to the fit
  they return. Plain data is kept exactly: base vectors and lists, data
  frames, factors, matrices, tables, dates and times. Anything else (an
  environment, a function, an S4 object, another class) is left out of the
  save with a warning of class `ferx_fit_user_field_dropped_warning`; loading
  decodes these objects as data and never deserializes R code from a bundle.
