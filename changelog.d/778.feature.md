- **`ferx_vpc_cif()`, a cumulative-incidence VPC for competing risks** (#778,
  [ferx-core #1887](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/1887)): every
  TTE endpoint of the model is a cause, and per stratum it returns the observed
  Aalen-Johansen cumulative incidence of each cause (equal to
  `survival::survfit()`'s multi-state fit) and the all-cause Kaplan-Meier curve,
  each beside the pointwise band of the replicates, with `print()` and `plot()`
  methods. The `ferx_vpc_tte()` / `ferx_vpc_kmmc()` refusal for several TTE
  endpoints and no `cmt` now names `ferx_vpc_cif()`.
