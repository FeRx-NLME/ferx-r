- **Engine fixes from ferx-core `48546b91..3cd15863`** (#719):

  - **A `.fitrx` bundle of a time-to-event model now loads its sdtab rows
    whole from ferx-core's own `predictions_tte.csv`**
    ([ferx-core #911](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/911)):
    a bundle written by ferx-core alone, without the R package's endpoint
    entry, now loads with its `COXSNELL` / `MARTINGALE` / `DEVIANCE` / `PIT`
    rows. A bundle saved from R counts each row once (#689).
  - **`EVID=2` compartment on/off records now work on `[odes]` models**
    ([ferx-core #2732](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2732)):
    `CMT=-N` used to be ignored, so collection compartments kept accumulating.
    It now zeroes and holds the compartment, as NONMEM does; a record the model
    cannot honour is refused.
  - An observation between a co-timed `EVID=3` reset and a dose is scored on
    the reset state, as NONMEM does. Before, the objective could be thousands of
    units off with no warning
    ([ferx-core #2728](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2728)).
  - `ferx_simulate_adaptive()` refuses a residual-error magnitude expression
    (`proportional(PROP * WT)`, `weight =`, `power(...)`) with
    `E_ADAPTIVE_UNSUPPORTED`. Before, the controller's assay drew from the bare
    sigma
    ([ferx-core #2741](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2741)).
  - Adaptive monitors (`observe`, a `[scaling]` readout reading `TIME`, and
    `auc_target` windows) read `TIME` as written after a reset that restarts
    it, instead of the engine clock
    ([ferx-core #2756](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2756)).
  - On binary or Markov-only data, a theta level block with no levels is no
    longer refused as "the data carries no observation rows", and
    `E_THETA_LEVEL_BINDING` says whether the read or the reader is the cause
    ([ferx-core #2623](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2623)).
  - The refusal for a `weight = ...` kappa referenced outside
    `[individual_parameters]` names every such block, in a stable order
    ([ferx-core #2624](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2624)).
