- **Engine fixes from ferx-core `86a05bd5..92e632bc`** (#706):

  - **The reported OFV no longer sits above the best objective the fit saw**
    ([ferx-core #2646](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2646)):
    the final cold EBE re-solve was accepted whenever it landed within
    `1e-3 * (1 + |OFV|)` of the best objective seen, so the optimizer's better
    EBEs could be dropped, and the reported OFV could be several units above
    them, with `converged = TRUE`. The bound is now an absolute 0.05 OFV
    units, also tested per subject. On FOCE, FOCEI, Laplace and adaptive
    quadrature (`n_agq > 1`) fits that had such a gap, `ofv`, the EBEs and
    `sdtab` change, and the fit now carries `W_EBE_START_DEPENDENT`, which
    names the subjects.
  - **A parameter that moves an `[odes]` `if` switch is no longer silently
    frozen** ([ferx-core #2649](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2649)):
    a THETA that reaches the equations only through a switch such as
    `if (tau > LAGT)` got a gradient of exactly zero and stayed at its initial
    value, sometimes with `converged = TRUE`. Such models now run on
    finite-difference gradients, with a warning naming the parameter, so their
    fits can take longer.
  - **`method = "imp"` no longer blows up at iteration 2 on correlated
    subjects** ([ferx-core #2650](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2650)):
    capping the proposal covariance at Omega now keeps it positive definite.
    IMP results change on any model where the cap was reached.
  - **`imp_proposal_df = "normal"` evaluates a real multivariate-normal
    proposal** ([ferx-core #2651](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2651)):
    under `imp_eval_only` and in the final marginal of an `imp` fit it gave
    `-2 log L = Inf` with ESS 0 for every subject.
  - **The importance-sampled marginal `-2 log L` no longer reads high on sparse
    subjects** ([ferx-core #2653](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2653)):
    each subject's proposal is adapted over pilot passes before the marginal in
    `fit$importance_sampling` is drawn, so reported marginals change and cost
    6x the draws by default. The new setting `imp_adapt_passes` (default 5;
    `0` restores the old proposal) sets the passes, and
    `fit$importance_sampling$adapt_passes` reports them.
