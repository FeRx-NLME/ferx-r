- **ferx now builds against ferx-core `0cbad3c2`**, up from `e3fb3d1e`.
  These engine changes reach R:

  - **One exact gradient for every likelihood**
    ([ferx-core #486](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/486),
    [#2093](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2093)). FOCE, FOCEI,
    Laplace and AGQ, with or without IOV, take their outer gradient from one
    reverse-mode (adjoint) identity at the EBEs, and the covariance step
    takes an exact R-matrix. It replaces the forward sensitivity provider and
    its 24-axis cap on theta + eta + kappa: there is no limit on theta, and
    up to 48 stacked random effects per IOV subject. Routes that used finite
    differences now get the exact gradient, among them Laplace and AGQ's
    covariance step, FOCE with proportional or combined error, a residual
    `correlation` line, nested variability levels, an eta-dependent
    `obs_scale` under LTBS and many-theta ODE models. The "24-axis cap"
    warning is gone. `fit$gradient_method_outer` keeps the label
    `"analytic (Dual2)"`. Standard errors on the routes that moved off
    finite differences change slightly, because the R-matrix is no longer a
    stencil. The inner EBE search also caps a runaway first step, and a fit
    minimising against trapped warm EBEs is now caught during the run and
    restarted, not only after it. Results move: of the 70 bundled example
    fits, 48 keep their OFV to 1e-6, and the rest move to a lower one.
    Most move by less than 0.2 (macOS arm64). The large moves:
    - `per_route_lag_absorption` reaches OFV -733.35, where it stopped at
      -514.28. Both pins give each point the same OFV, so this is a lower
      optimum of the same objective.
    - `warfarin_dcm` reaches -1190.33, where it stopped at -1185.46. It
      keeps `covariance_method = "r"`, which now fails on the network
      weights' flat Hessian diagonal; it used to switch to `"s"`, which also
      failed.
    - `warfarin_sde` (finite differences, BOBYQA) finds a trap and resumes
      to -280.57, from -276.69. It now reports `converged = FALSE`: at the
      end, its warm-started EBEs score 0.42 OFV better than a cold re-solve,
      so the final check refuses to call the plateau converged. The fit says
      so in `W_EBE_START_DEPENDENT`, whose remedy is a larger
      `inner_maxiter` or `inner_restarts`.
    - `adaptive_vanco_loading`'s covariance step, a regularized matrix with
      unbounded variance inflation, now fails on the residual error's zero
      Hessian diagonal.
  - **A `kappa` model with a residual `correlation` line takes the analytic
    outer gradient and covariance step**
    ([ferx-core #2101](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2101)).
    `mbma_placebo` with `correlation car1(RHO) by OCC` reaches the same OFV,
    89.4512784, from RHO = 0.01 and from 0.6. It now reports
    `gradient_method_outer = "analytic (Dual2)"` and fits in 0.3 s, where it
    took 34 s on finite differences. The two warnings about finite
    differences on this route are gone. SE(RHO) moves from 0.06438 to
    0.06454. With a residual `weight =` (as `mbma_placebo` has), the inner
    EBE search on this route still uses finite differences, and
    `gradient_method_inner` says so.
  - **A nested `level` column is no longer reported as an undeclared
    covariate**
    ([ferx-core #2166](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2166),
    [#2175](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2175)). With a
    `[covariates]` block, `level ARM by ARM in STUDY` no longer warns that
    `ARM` is used but not declared. A level column that a model expression
    also reads is still reported.
  - **Every "Covariance step failed" warning is classed `covariance_failed`**
    ([ferx-core #2158](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2158),
    [#2176](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2176)). A profiled
    fit's "the profile solve did not converge at the estimates" used to be
    filed under `convergence` in `fit$warnings_structured` and
    `ferx_get_warnings()`. ferx already gave it the covariance guidance
    rather than the advice to re-fit from new initial values, and still does
    for a fit made before this version.
  - **A constrained `theta NAME[cols]` block with no bounds written defaults
    to `(-1e9, 1e9)`**
    ([ferx-core #2174](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2174),
    [#2178](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2178)). Its
    coefficients are contrasts and have no sign, so `(0.0)` and `(0.0) FIX`
    used to be refused ("constrained level bounds must include 0"). Both now
    bind and fit as `(0.0, -1e9, 1e9)` does. Written bounds must now hold 0
    strictly inside them: `(0.0, 0.0, 5.0)` used to hold every coefficient to
    one sign, and is now refused, naming the side and the fix. A `FIX` block
    is exempt.
  - **`ferx_covariance()` and `ferx_sir()` check the population against the
    model's theta levels on a fit without a population fingerprint**
    ([ferx-core #1793](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/1793),
    [#1795](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/1795),
    [#1798](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/1798),
    [#2178](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2178)). On a fit
    saved before fingerprints, or with a stale one, data never bound, partly
    bound or bound for other levels used to run on a `NaN` objective or at
    the wrong levels. They are now refused with `E_THETA_LEVELS_DATA_UNBOUND`
    or `E_THETA_LEVELS_DATA_MISMATCH` on `e$code`. Data that list a level
    column but carry it on no subject are told they were never bound.
  - **A BOBYQA fit is polished before it is called converged**
    ([ferx-core #1782](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/1782),
    [#2170](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2170)). This applies
    to `optimizer = "bobyqa"`, and to `"auto"` when it resolves to BOBYQA. A
    stop on the relative `outer_ftol` / `outer_xtol` used to report
    `converged = TRUE`. It is now followed by up to four tighter legs from
    the best point, and the last one decides `converged`. If that budget runs
    out, the fit reports `converged = FALSE` with a warning. These fits take
    more evaluations, and their estimates can move to a lower optimum.
  - **Adaptive dosing honours `[scaling] obs_scale`**
    ([ferx-core #2129](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2129),
    [#2142](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2142), in the
    previous pin but not announced). This covers
    `ferx_simulate_adaptive()`'s trajectories, the signal the controller
    reads, its assay error and the `auc_target` exposure. On a model with
    `obs_scale = V` they used to come back as the bare amount, `V` times too
    high. They now use the scale `ferx_predict()` and `ferx_simulate()` use.
  - **ODE compartment states seed `init()` from the subject's first record**
    ([ferx-core #2133](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2133),
    [#2141](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2141), in the
    previous pin but not announced). This covers the sdtab state columns and
    `[derived]` `compartments[i]`, at the first record of any kind and at
    each EVID=3/4 reset's own row, as IPRED and NONMEM do. A subject whose
    first record is a dose or EVID=2 row with a different covariate got
    states off by the seed's covariate ratio.
