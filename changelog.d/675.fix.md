- **A `[mixture]` fit that records its per-class override values only in its
  packed estimate is no longer refused** (#675): `ferx_simulate(fit = )`,
  `ferx_calc_npde()`, `ferx_simulate_adaptive(fit = )`,
  `ferx_simulate_with_uncertainty()` and the TTE VPCs refused such a fit (a
  `.fitrx` saved before `fit$mixture_overrides` existed) with
  `E_FIT_INPUT_MISSING`, while `ferx_sir()` and `ferx_covariance()` accepted
  it. They now read the overrides from `fit$packed_estimate`, and give the
  same result as on a fit that records them. `ferx_update_model()` on such a
  fit now seeds the `omega(k)` / `sigma(k)` lines from it too, instead of
  leaving them as written with a warning.
