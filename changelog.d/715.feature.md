- **Engine features from ferx-core `92e632bc..0dabda75`** (#715):

  - **`imp_max_time` and `impmap_max_time` settings**
    ([ferx-core #2687](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2687)):
    a wall-clock limit in seconds on the IMP / IMPMAP iterations. A fit that
    reaches it stops after the current iteration, keeps its estimate, and
    reports `converged = FALSE` with a warning. IMP and IMPMAP also stop
    rebuilding each subject's event schedule per draw, which speeds up models
    with time-varying covariates without changing results.
  - **Per-subject dose expressions in `[adaptive_dosing]`**
    ([ferx-core #944](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/944)):
    `start_dose`, each side of `dose_bounds` and each rung of `levels` can be
    an expression over THETA and data covariates, e.g. `start_dose = 15 * WT`,
    evaluated once per subject at the first decision. A bad value is refused
    with `E_ADAPTIVE_DOSE_VALUE` before any subject runs.
  - **A fit says when its `[odes]` right-hand side is not native code**
    ([ferx-core #2681](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2681)):
    an informational `W_ODE_RHS_INTERPRETED` (or `W_ODE_RHS_NOT_NATIVE`) row in
    `fit$warning_details`, category `ode_rhs_route`, names the construct that
    keeps the model on the slower bytecode interpreter, and
    `ferx_get_warnings()` prints guidance for it.
  - **Inline `if`, `min`, `max` and `clamp` in `[odes]` are compiled**
    ([ferx-core #2680](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2680)):
    they no longer drop the model to the interpreter, with bit-identical
    results; `warfarin_ode_time` fits about 3.5x faster.
