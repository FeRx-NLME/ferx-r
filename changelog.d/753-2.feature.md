- **`ferx_simulate_with_uncertainty()` takes a `horizon`** (#753,
  [ferx-core #2003](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2003)), the
  administrative censoring time of every draw, as in `ferx_simulate()`. A
  repeated-event (`type = rtte`) or drug-driven (joint PK-TTE) model needs one
  and was refused here before; it can now be simulated with parameter
  uncertainty.
