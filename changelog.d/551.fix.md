- `print()`'s check of `kappa_param_types`, `eta_param_types` and
  `covariance_status` now takes the known random-effect scales and covariance
  statuses from the compiled engine glue that reads them, instead of a copy
  in R, so a scale or status a newer ferx-core adds no longer makes `print()`
  refuse a valid fit with a `ferx_fit_field_error` ([#551](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-r/issues/551)).
