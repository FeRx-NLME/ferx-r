- **`print()` of a fit with an unreadable OMEGA or OMEGA_IOV field now refuses
  before printing anything.** (#567) The `ferx_fit_field_error` for a scale
  vector of the wrong length, an unknown scale or an unreadable covariance
  status used to come after the header and the THETA table were written, so
  `capture.output()` and knitr kept a truncated report. The checks now run
  first: the report is printed whole or not at all.
