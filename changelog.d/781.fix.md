- **A function called `data.frame` in your global environment no longer breaks
  ferx** (#780): `ferx_predict()` (with or without `fit =`),
  `ferx_predict_survival()`, `ferx_simulate_adaptive()` and `ferx_calc_npde()`
  (including its time-to-event scores) used to stop with a Rust panic that
  printed the whole result table. They now return the same tables as before,
  whatever `data.frame` means in the global environment.
