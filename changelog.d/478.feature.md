- **`plot_param_dist()` plots the distribution of each random-effect
  parameter** (#478): per ETA-bearing individual parameter, a histogram of the
  individual estimates with an EBE rug, the population density the model
  implies (log-normal, additive or logit, and the ferx-core shape transforms
  `boxcox`/`tdist`/`johndraper` once the engine reports their shape), a dashed
  log-normal reference with the same omega, and an optional Q-Q panel of the
  EBEs against N(0, omega) that quotes the eta-shrinkage. `conddist = TRUE`
  uses conditional-distribution draws instead of point EBEs. The new
  `fit$eta_param_info` reports which individual parameter each ETA enters, and
  survives `ferx_save_fit()`/`ferx_load_fit()`.
