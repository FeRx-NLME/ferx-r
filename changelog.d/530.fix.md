- **`ferx_covariance()`, `ferx_sir()` and `ferx_calc_npde()` no longer refuse a
  fit whose re-read data are its own** (#530): on a fit that records a
  population fingerprint but no reader settings, the legacy selection guard
  credited a dropped row to the first clause matching it, so a call-time
  `ignore =` whose rows the model file's `accept =` drops anyway was named as
  lost. The guard now reads the data the way the step will and compares it with
  the fit's fingerprint; the same population runs, and only a fit with no
  fingerprint to compare is refused on the clause names alone.
