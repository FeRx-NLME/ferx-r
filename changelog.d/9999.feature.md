- **New fit fields and engine features from ferx-core `3cd15863..7c63c7a5`** (#9999):

  - **`fit$status`, `fit$status_label` and `fit$status_reasons`: one verdict on
    whether a fit can be used as it stands**
    ([ferx-core #2799](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2799)).
    `status` is `"converged"`, `"converged_with_issues"` or `"not_converged"`,
    and `status_reasons` says why, in the engine's order. The engine computes
    it from the fit on every read, so `ferx_covariance()` refreshes it and a
    `.fitrx` bundle written before it existed loads with one. `print()` shows
    it on its `STATUS` line (see Breaking changes).
  - **`fit$held_at_bound`: every parameter the covariance step reports no
    standard error for**
    ([ferx-core #2797](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2797)),
    as a data frame of `parameter`, `kind`, `index` and `reason`
    (`"profiled_bound"` or `"collapsed_at_guard"`). `fit$profiled_levels_at_bound`
    is deprecated in its favour.
  - **`ferx_simulate_adaptive()` applies a residual-error magnitude to the
    controller's `Dv` reading**
    ([ferx-core #2741](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2741)):
    a magnitude expression (`proportional(PROP * WT)`), `weight =`,
    `power(...)` or a magnitude on a `CMT=N:` line no longer refuses the run
    with `E_ADAPTIVE_UNSUPPORTED`. The reading's variance is the one
    `ferx_simulate()` gives an observation at the decision. A magnitude that is
    not positive and finite there fails with `E_RUV_MAGNITUDE_NONPOSITIVE`.
  - **`ferx_simulate_adaptive()` runs on data with `EVID=2` compartment on/off
    rows** (`CMT=-N` / `CMT=N`), which it refused before
    ([ferx-core #2753](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2753)). A
    controller dose into an off compartment turns it back on, and a monitor on
    an off compartment reads 0.
  - **`[adaptive_dosing]` dose expressions can read a subject's individual
    parameters when they are known**
    ([ferx-core #944](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/944)): with
    `start_dose = 400 * CL`, each subject whose eta is supplied through `etas`
    is dosed from its own CL. A subject whose eta is drawn is refused
    (`E_ADAPTIVE_DOSE_KNOWLEDGE`) unless the block sets
    `dose_knowledge = oracle`, a perfect-information comparison arm that warns
    `W_ADAPTIVE_DOSE_ORACLE` and flags those subjects in the new
    `$metrics$DOSE_ORACLE` column.
  - **`round_to = <step>` in `[adaptive_dosing]`** puts every dose on a grid:
    the start dose and each percentage step are rounded to the nearest multiple
    (ties up) and clamped to the multiples inside `dose_bounds`. It cannot be
    combined with `levels`, and bounds that hold no multiple are refused with
    `E_ADAPTIVE_DOSE_VALUE`
    ([ferx-core #944](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/944)).
  - A `threads` count, or a `RAYON_NUM_THREADS` value, above the available CPUs
    now warns that the workers time-share the cores
    ([ferx-core #1811](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/1811)).
  - Faster fits: exact linear `[odes]` models propagate through an
    eigen-decomposition (1.6-3.4x fewer instructions, same estimates;
    [ferx-core #2747](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2747)).
    Doses after a subject's last observation are left out of the likelihood, so
    long-treatment ODE fits can run an order of magnitude faster with the same
    objective; fits on time-varying-covariate and IOV routes can move around
    the ninth significant digit
    ([ferx-core #2778](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2778)).
    Cold EBE solves under a strongly correlated `block_omega` are faster too
    (FOCEI 191 s to 13 s on a melphalan model;
    [ferx-core #2788](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2788)).
