- **Engine fixes with ferx-core `a8f5f056..13e6c8bf`** (#777):

  - A closed-form fit with an `SS=1` dose whose lag is longer than `II` gets
    the right gradient, and its compartment states (`[derived]` columns) follow
    the predictions; such a fit used to stay at its initial estimates
    ([ferx-core #1353](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/1353),
    [#3028](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/3028)).
  - A trough listed before a co-timed dose reads pre-dose on an ODE model when
    a lag arrival lands one ulp below the dose
    ([ferx-core #2534](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2534)).
  - `sqrt` in `[odes]` no longer sends subjects to finite differences (a QSS
    TMDD fit: 6.8x faster, same OFV)
    ([ferx-core #3002](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/3002)).
  - A `[data_selection]` rule that excludes one record no longer drops a whole
    binary, time-to-event or count subject
    ([ferx-core #3148](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/3148)).
  - Finite differences on a small theta (a covariate or Hill exponent declared
    near 0) are resolved in the AGQ / Laplace score and the profiled covariance
    step too, so such a profiled step no longer fails as not positive definite
    ([ferx-core #2678](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2678)).
  - `covariance_method = "s"` says why `S` is singular: the parameters whose
    score is zero or collinear, or the rank on a design with fewer subjects
    than free parameters, and advises `"r"` / `"rsr"` only for the design
    case; `ferx_get_warnings()` follows it
    ([ferx-core #1780](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/1780)).
  - An unconverged `method = "gn"` fit on fewer subjects than free parameters
    says why it stalled and points to `method = "gn_hybrid"`, and
    `ferx_get_warnings()` no longer suggests other initial values or more
    starts for it
    ([ferx-core #2438](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2438)).
  - `[error_model]` refusals name what the model file says: a `weight =` that
    reads `IPRED` is reported as a `weight =` expression, and a
    `[structural_model]` name in a magnitude as a prediction-side name, not as
    an undeclared covariate
    ([ferx-core #3121](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/3121)).
  - The `ode_method = "auto"` escalation note no longer offers `rk45` as an
    equal choice, and neither does `ferx_get_warnings()`: name a stiff method
    (`rodas5p`, `rodas4`) to skip the stiffness probe
    ([ferx-core #3154](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/3154),
    #775).
  - An ODE inner solve whose mode ties a neighbouring point keeps the mode's
    predictions
    ([ferx-core #3042](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/3042)); a
    cancelled fit returns promptly during a reconverged finite-difference
    gradient
    ([ferx-core #2776](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2776)); a
    Laplace fit reports its stage optimizer and gradient route
    ([ferx-core #3123](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/3123)).
  - Faster, with the same estimates: IOV subjects whose random effects stay at
    zero and profiled subjects with a zero mode are not re-scanned
    ([ferx-core #2900](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2900),
    [#2899](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2899)); FOCE with many
    observations per subject past the Woodbury gate (100 infusions per subject:
    52 s to 4.9 s)
    ([ferx-core #2988](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2988)); the
    IOV EBE search scores value and gradient in one pass
    ([ferx-core #3088](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/3088)).
