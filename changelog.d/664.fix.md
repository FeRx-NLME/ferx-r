- **`ferx_model_validate()` reports informational rows as `info`** (#664): the
  engine's `W_LEVEL_SUMMARY` per-level counts now arrive with severity `"info"`
  (ferx-core #2427). They are printed as `info` and no longer turn the result
  into "VALID (with warnings)"; a model whose only rows are informational
  validates as `VALID`.
