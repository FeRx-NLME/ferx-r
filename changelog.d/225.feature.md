- **Regular-vine copula random effects (`omega_dist = "vine"`)** (#225):
  `ferx_fit(..., method = "saem", settings = list(omega_dist = "vine"))`
  estimates zero-mean flexible marginals (`normal`, `student_t`, `skew_normal`,
  and Azzalini `skew_t`, or pinned Gaussian marginals via
  `vine_marginals = "gaussian"`) jointly with a Dissmann regular-vine copula
  over `independence`, `gaussian`, `student_t`, `frank`, and rotated `clayton`
  / `gumbel` pair-copulas ($0^\circ, 90^\circ, 180^\circ, 270^\circ$). Fitted
  marginals and vine trees are returned on `fit$vine_copula`, the QMC
  importance-sampled marginal `-2 log L` is reported on `fit$ofv` and
  `fit$vine_corrected_ofv`, `ferx_vine_density()` evaluates 1-D marginal and
  2-D/joint densities, `ferx_simulate()` / `ferx_calc_npde()` draw from the
  fitted regular vine, and `ferx_example()` ships `vine_tail_iv`,
  `vine_tail_iv_gaussian`, and `warfarin_vine`.
