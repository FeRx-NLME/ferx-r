- **New `ferx_vpc_kmmc()`, the Kaplan-Meier mean covariate VPC** (#754,
  [ferx-core #1886](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/1886)). It
  checks whether a single-event TTE model captures how a baseline covariate
  drives the hazard: at each step of the Kaplan-Meier curve it takes the
  covariate's mean over the subjects still at risk (or, with `level`, the
  share at one value), and returns the observed curve beside the band over
  `n_sim` replicates, per stratum. It takes every `ferx_vpc_tte()` argument
  plus `covariate`, `level` and `min_at_risk` (default 10), works at a fit's
  parameters with `fit =`, and has `print()` and `plot()` methods. A covariate
  that changes within a subject raises `W_VPC_KMMC_TIME_VARYING_COVARIATE`.
