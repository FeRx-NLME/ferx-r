- **Engine fixes from ferx-core `3f87757e..0703293c`** (#686). Predictions,
  OFVs or estimates change for the models each item names:

  - **On ODE models, an observation listed before a co-timed dose reads
    pre-dose**, as NONMEM does
    ([ferx-core #2124](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2124),
    [#2485](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2485)): at the
    subject's first record time, at its last observation, and (for times up
    to 8) on `ferx_simulate_adaptive()`'s time-varying path, the dose was
    applied first, so the trough read post-dose. This reached
    `ferx_predict()`, `ferx_fit()` (objective and gradient), the sdtab state
    columns, `[derived]`, the survival hazard and adaptive dosing.
  - **`ferx_simulate_adaptive()` no longer refuses an observation listed
    before a co-timed `EVID = 2` row** under time-varying covariates or IOV
    ([ferx-core #2123](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2123),
    [#2485](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2485)); it reads the
    observation before the row.
  - **`ferx_simulate_adaptive()` with IOV or time-varying covariates: a trough
    listed before a co-timed pre-scheduled dose reads pre-dose**, and so does
    a decision at that time; both read post-dose, off by the dose
    ([ferx-core #2532](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2532),
    [#2533](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2533)).
  - **Adaptive dosing with an `ADDL` base regimen**
    ([ferx-core #2371](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2371),
    [#2528](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2528),
    [#2530](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2530)): under IOV an
    implied dose takes the kappa of the record that governs it, as
    `ferx_simulate()` takes its `OCC` (the trajectory drifted 5.6% from
    `ferx_simulate()` on the regression fixture), and a decision no longer
    reads covariates an implied dose carries from a later record (its
    context, readout and the `F` of the dose it injects).
  - **`method = "laplace"` is the Laplace approximation exactly**
    ([ferx-core #2538](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2538),
    [#2526](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2526)): a relative
    1e-6 diagonal jitter on the eta Hessian raised every subject's objective;
    `profile_mbma` now matches NONMEM's `METHOD=COND LAPLACE` to 4e-8 (was
    9e-5). Laplace and AGQ OFVs move by about 1e-6 relative.
  - **SAEM takes the closed-form update for an additive typical value**
    written on its own line (`E0 = TVE0 + ETA_E0`)
    ([ferx-core #2540](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2540),
    [#2545](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2545)): SAEM used to
    stop 1-11 OFV above the optimum on `mbma_placebo`, with omega inflated up
    to 6x. A typical value summed with other terms in one line is still not
    mu-referenced, and the SAEM warning now says to split it. Single-population
    fits only.
  - **A `saem`, `imp`, `impmap` or `vi` fit whose estimate is not a
    stationary point now warns `W_COV_NON_STATIONARY_ESTIMATE`** instead of
    reporting standard errors silently, when the Newton decrement of the
    covariance step's objective exceeds 1 OFV unit; the SEs are unchanged
    ([ferx-core #2541](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2541),
    [#2547](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2547)). The warning
    after these methods now suggests a chain the parser accepts
    (`method = [saem, focei]` in the model file, `method = c("saem", "focei")`
    in R), says that the polish moves the estimates, and offers `sir = true`
    for intervals
    ([ferx-core #2536](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2536),
    [#2537](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2537)).
