- **Clearer warnings when a gradient-driven fit stops without converging**
  (#740, ferx-core #2713). The outer-gradient fallback warning now counts the
  subjects that fell back per cause (an inner Hessian that was singular or
  indefinite, versus a subject no analytic recorder served), and a fit that
  stops unconverged says how many subjects use the fixed-EBE fallback at its
  estimates, how far that gradient is from the exact one, and the rerun that
  settles it: `settings = list(reconverge_gradient_interval = 1L)`.
