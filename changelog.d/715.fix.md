- **Engine fixes from ferx-core `92e632bc..0dabda75`** (#715):

  - **IMP and IMPMAP fits no longer report `converged = TRUE` on a collapsed
    run** ([ferx-core #2652](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2652)):
    a non-finite or diverged importance-sampling marginal demotes the fit with
    `W_NONFINITE_MARGINAL`, and a final marginal far above the lowest one the
    iterations reached demotes it with `W_MCEM_OBJECTIVE_ROSE`. An
    `imp_eval_only` readout gets the first check, and a chain ending in an
    `agq_eval_only` readout now publishes the marginal and gets both.
  - **A starting point the EBE convergence guard rejects is re-solved warm
    before it is judged**
    ([ferx-core #2682](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2682)):
    when too many subjects' inner loops failed from the cold start
    (`max_unconverged_frac`), every later trial failed the same way and the fit
    walked toward the middle of its bounds. With `verbose = TRUE` a rejected
    evaluation now prints as a rejection with its unconverged count instead of
    an `OFV = 1000000...` line.
  - **`imp_eval_only` on IOV models scores the likelihood IMP/IMPMAP
    estimate** ([ferx-core #2627](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2627)):
    with `imp_adapt_passes = 0` the readout left out FREM covariate rows and
    TTE / binary / categorical endpoints and ignored `imp_defensive_alpha`.
