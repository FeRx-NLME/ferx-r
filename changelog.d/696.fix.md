- `ferx_xpose()`, `ferx_save_fit()` and `ferx_calc_npde()` now split
  `fit$sdtab` into its Gaussian, discrete and time-to-event rows with one
  shared rule (#696). Two edge cases change. `ferx_calc_npde()` on a fit
  whose `subject_summary` counts are missing or do not add up now finds the
  time-to-event rows by their `COXSNELL` value instead of refusing the fit.
  `ferx_save_fit()` on a fit whose `subject_summary` has no `n_obs_discrete`,
  `ID` or `ofv_contribution` column now places each row's subject ID from the
  counts instead of from the ID run-length.
