  survival curve at its fit's EBEs** (#1885): `survival`, `cum_hazard`,
  `hazard`, `cif` and the median/mean survival time at `fit$ebe_etas` instead
  of eta = 0. `"population"` (the default) keeps the eta = 0 curve. Every
  subject in `data` needs a row in `fit$ebe_etas`, so pass the fitted data or a
  subset of it; a `[mixture]` model is refused
  ([ferx-core #1885](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/1885),
