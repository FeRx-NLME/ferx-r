- **New with ferx-core `a8f5f056..13e6c8bf`** (#777):

  - `settings = list(ode_ofv_check = FALSE)` skips the post-fit OFV settlement
    check on an ODE fit (`W_ODE_OFV_SETTLEMENT_UNCHECKED` then says so, and
    `ferx_get_warnings()` says that it was asked for)
    ([ferx-core #3157](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/3157),
    #774).
  - `settings = list(inner_kick = TRUE)` kicks every inner (EBE) solve to
    reach a second mode a clipped prediction puts a kink away, and escapes a
    stall on the kink; off by default (2-20x slower on four fast ODE examples
    without a kink), and
    recorded in `fit$scoring_settings`
    ([ferx-core #3159](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/3159)).
  - A residual-magnitude expression may read `IPRED`, e.g.
    `DV ~ additive(EPS * A * exp(B * IPRED) / sqrt(NIND))`, with analytic
    gradients under FOCE, FOCEI and Laplace, validated against NONMEM
    ([ferx-core #3121](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/3121)).
  - `ferx_simulate_adaptive()`'s `doses`, `decisions` and `metrics` tables gain
    a `REP` column after `SIM`, the 1-based assay replicate; `1` on every row
    until replicates can be requested
    ([ferx-core #946](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/946)).
  - The TTE VPCs warn with `W_VPC_EMPTY_STRATUM` when a stratum has no band
    at any grid time, naming why: `ferx_vpc_kmmc()` on any grid,
    `ferx_vpc_tte()` and `ferx_vpc_rtte()` only with a numeric `grid` (the
    default `"auto"` and `"all_steps"` grids never raise it). The print method gives advice
    under it, and the plot writes a note in that panel instead of drawing the
    observed curve alone
    ([ferx-core #2947](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2947)).
