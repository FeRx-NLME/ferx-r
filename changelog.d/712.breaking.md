- **`ferx_save_fit()` writes the bytes ferx-core made, and old fits are not
  supported** (#712, [ferx-core #2693](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2693)):
  R's own `.fitrx` writer is gone, so a bundle saved from R is the one the
  ferx-core command line writes and reads (`ferx summary run1.fitrx`). A fit's
  bytes (`fit$bundle`) are the fit: a change made to its list by hand is not
  saved and does not reach `ferx_covariance()`, `ferx_sir()` or
  `ferx_calc_npde()`. A fit with no bytes, such as one from an `.rds` written by
  an older ferx, is refused by all four with an error of class
  `ferx_no_bundle_error` saying to refit, and old `.fitrx` bundles still fail
  with a version error. There is no fallback or migration.
