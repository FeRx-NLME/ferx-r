- **Engine features from ferx-core `bbda824e..aa5f3b6e`** (#699):

  - **A profiled theta level block can be read by a `[binary_model] logit`
    predictor**
    ([ferx-core #2111](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2111),
    [#2604](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2604)):
    `logit = TVL + PLACEBO[STUDY, TIME] + ETA + EMAX * TRT` now profiles its
    placebo block instead of being refused with `E_PROFILE_SCOPE`, under
    `foce`, `focei`, `laplace` and adaptive quadrature. A model with a
    time-to-event or Markov endpoint is still refused, by a message naming
    that endpoint.
  - **`fit$stationary_start`**: `TRUE` when a fit that did not leave its
    start was restarted from a perturbation of it and came back to the same
    objective, i.e. a warm start at an optimum rather than a stall. It
    travels through `ferx_save_fit()` / `ferx_load_fit()`
    ([ferx-core #2506](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2506),
    [#2524](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2524)).
  - **Faster inner solves at a tight `inner_tol`** and a **faster covariance
    step on models with `FIX` parameters**: a one-compartment IIV+IOV FOCEI
    fit drops from about 15 s to 1.6 s, and the analytic R-matrix skips held
    coordinates, with standard errors unchanged bit for bit
    ([ferx-core #2497](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2497),
    [#2597](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2597)).
