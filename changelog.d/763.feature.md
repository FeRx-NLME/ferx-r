- **`ferx_simulate_adaptive()` can propagate a fit's parameter uncertainty**
  (#763, ferx-core #946): `uncertainty = list(method = "asymptotic" | "sir",
  n_draws = )`, together with `fit =`, draws `n_draws` parameter sets from the
  fit's covariance matrix or its kept SIR samples and simulates every subject
  and replicate under each. Every row of `trajectories`, `doses`, `decisions`
  and `metrics` is tagged with its `DRAW`, and the new `parameters` table holds
  each draw's theta, Omega, sigma and kappa, named as `fit$cov_matrix` names its
  rows (no rows without uncertainty). Draw 1 is unchanged by `n_draws`. Dose
  expressions that read only theta are evaluated at the fit's estimate, while
  a `dose_knowledge = oracle` subject is dosed from its own draw's parameters.
  Warning and refusal messages name the replicate as "(draw d, sim s)" only
  with uncertainty. Refused: `uncertainty` without `fit`, together with `etas`
  (`E_SUPPLIED_ETAS`), `method = "asymptotic"` on a fit without
  `fit$cov_matrix`, and `method = "sir"` on a fit without kept SIR samples.
