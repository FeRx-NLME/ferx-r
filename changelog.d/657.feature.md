- **ferx now builds against ferx-core `81468734`**, up from `d66c0c51`
  (#657). These engine changes reach R:

  - **A `profile`d theta level block now fits together with a residual
    `correlation cs|ar1|car1(RHO) by ...` line**
    ([ferx-core #2110](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2110),
    [#2390](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2390)), including the
    MBMA shape of an arm kappa with `correlation car1(RHO) by OCC`. Before,
    `ferx_fit()` refused the pair with `E_PROFILE_SCOPE`. The profiled fit
    reaches the unprofiled fit's optimum: on `mbma_placebo` with
    `correlation car1(RHO) by OCC` the OFV is 89.4513 either way, and
    `ferx_covariance()` repeats the profiled fit's standard errors bit for
    bit, also after `ferx_save_fit()` / `ferx_load_fit()`. As for every
    profiled fit, only `covariance_method = "r"` is available.
  - **A profiled fit's covariance step guards and times its re-solve of the
    levels** ([ferx-core #2214](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2214),
    [#2388](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2388)). A re-solve
    that leaves too many subjects' EBEs unconverged (`max_unconverged_frac`),
    or rejects one at its inner start, now fails the step with a
    `covariance_failed` warning that gives the counts, instead of feeding the
    standard errors. `ferx_get_warnings()` gives each form its own advice
    (a larger `inner_maxiter` for the unconverged fraction; for a start
    rejection, which no setting moves, a check of the estimates and the
    subject's data), not the generic identifiability text. The new
    `fit$covariance_wall_time_secs` reports the covariance step's wall-clock
    time, the re-solve included; `ferx_covariance()` refreshes it and
    `ferx_save_fit()` / `ferx_load_fit()` keep it.
  - **A profiled fit now reports subjects that fell back from the analytic
    outer gradient to finite differences**, with the warning an unprofiled fit
    gives, worded for the reconverged gradient those subjects take
    ([ferx-core #2394](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2394)).
    Before, a profiled fit said nothing.
  - **A finite-difference gradient probe the EBE guard rejects is no longer
    differenced as a value**
    ([ferx-core #2378](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2378),
    [#2389](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2389)). The
    reconverged FD outer gradient and the reported final gradient scored such
    a probe at the objective's `1e20` wall, and the IOV per-subject salvage
    gradient scored an unusable subject at the `1e20` sentinel, so one
    gradient component could come out near `1e23`. A rejected probe now
    counts as missing: that component takes the one-sided difference from
    the other side, or is zero when both sides (or the centre) are rejected.
  - **Bayes's final EBE pass on an IOV model starts each occasion at its
    posterior-mean kappa**, not at 0
    ([ferx-core #2377](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/issues/2377),
    [#2384](https://github.com/FeRx-NLME/ferx-core/pull/2384)). The modes are
    the same (to about 1e-9).
