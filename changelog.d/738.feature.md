- **`ferx_vpc_tte()`: Kaplan-Meier VPC for a single-event TTE endpoint**
  (#738, ferx-core #1886): simulates the observed design `n_sim` times and
  returns, per stratum (a covariate, or `by_dose = TRUE`), the observed
  Kaplan-Meier curve with its Nelson-Aalen cumulative hazard (matching
  `survival::survfit` row for row) beside the pointwise 5%-95% band of the
  replicate curves, with the replicates censored like the data
  (`censoring = "observed"`, or `"horizon"`). `fit =` simulates at the fitted
  parameters. `plot()` draws it with `vpc::plot_vpc()` when the vpc package is
  installed, otherwise with base graphics. Inputs the curve would be wrong on
  (competing risks without `cmt`, repeated events, left truncation, interval
  censoring, too few replicates) are refused with `e$code == "E_VPC_TTE_INPUT"`.
