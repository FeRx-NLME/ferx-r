- **A VI fit carries its variational-inference result as `fit$vi`, and
  `ferx_save_fit()` / `ferx_load_fit()` keep it** (#508, part of #507).
  `fit$vi` holds the -2 x ELBO bound and its data / KL halves, convergence,
  the variational family, the ELBO and objective traces, each subject's
  posterior means (`eta_means`, keyed by `ID`), posterior covariances
  (`eta_covs`, with eta-name dimnames) and per-occasion IOV means
  (`kappa_means`), and the engine's diagnostics (`n_fd_subjects`,
  `elbo_tightness_ratio`, `superseded_by`). The `.fitrx` bundle stores it in
  ferx-core's own `vi` slot (ferx-core #2058), so ferx-core loads an R VI
  bundle with its VI result and R loads the engine's; it used to be dropped
  on every round trip. A bundle whose VI posterior names a different subject
  than `ebes.csv` at the same position is refused, as the engine refuses it.
